# Layan SVG Asset Pack — Folders 04–09

24 true-vector SVG files. No raster images are embedded.

## Developer rules
- SVGs use a 256×256 viewBox and transparent canvas.
- Navigation icons: render at 24–28 px; active state may use Primary #7776D8.
- Diabetes objects: use in logging, education and detail screens; not as medical-status indicators.
- Food icons: category/logging illustrations only.
- Status icons: always pair with glucose number + text. Never communicate low/high by color alone.
- Decorations: decorative only; never behind glucose values, insulin doses, graphs or warnings.
- Brand: these are simplified vector marks. Detailed Layan character artwork should remain a raster illustration rather than fake-traced SVG.
